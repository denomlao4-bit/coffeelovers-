Explore Coffee Varieties

Coffee culture has evolved for centuries across different regions. Every coffee type carries unique flavors, traditions, and brewing styles.

Espresso

Origin: Italy

Espresso is a strong coffee brewed by forcing hot water through finely-ground coffee beans.

Flavor

Strong, bold, rich, slightly bitter.

Recipe

  • 18g finely ground coffee
  • Hot pressurized water
  • 25–30 seconds extraction

Latte

Origin: Italy

Latte is a milk-based coffee known for its smooth and creamy texture.

Flavor

Soft, creamy, mild coffee taste.

Recipe

  • 1 espresso shot
  • Steamed milk
  • Light milk foam

Cappuccino

Origin: Italy

Cappuccino is a balanced coffee drink with equal parts espresso, milk, and foam.

Flavor

Foamy, creamy, rich espresso taste.

Recipe

  • 1 espresso shot
  • Steamed milk
  • Thick milk foam

Cortado

Origin: Spain

Cortado balances espresso with a small amount of warm milk.

Flavor

Balanced, smooth, less acidic.

Recipe

  • 1 espresso shot
  • Equal amount of warm milk

Affogato

Origin: Italy

Affogato is a dessert coffee made with espresso poured over ice cream.

Flavor

Sweet, creamy, rich coffee dessert.

Recipe

  • 1 shot hot espresso
  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream

Iced Coffee

Origin: Global

Iced coffee is a refreshing cold version of brewed coffee.

Flavor

Cold, refreshing, slightly sweet.

Recipe

  • Fresh brewed coffee
  • Ice cubes
  • Optional milk or syrup
And many more coffee varieties exist across different cultures, each with unique taste and tradition.
